
# Session Completion Hypothesis

Status:

CANDIDATE

Classification:

Hypothesis

---

## Hypothesis SCH-001

Users working within bounded time windows may derive greater value from completing one coherent unit of work than from maximizing throughput across multiple partially completed efforts.

---

## Motivation

During corridor work, discussion of available session time suggested that the perceived quality of a collaboration session may depend not only on total progress, but also on whether the session reaches a psychologically coherent stopping point.

This observation emerged independently of any specific implementation discussion.

---

## Candidate Implications

If supported by future evidence, Matilda may eventually:

- Estimate whether sufficient time remains to complete a coherent unit of work.

- Recommend wrapping up rather than beginning a new corridor when completion appears unlikely.

- Optimize for continuity and completion without reducing reasoning quality.

- Preserve unfinished work through explicit continuation artifacts when continuation is recommended.

---

## Evidence Required

Future investigation should determine:

- Whether this pattern generalizes across users.

- Whether satisfaction correlates more strongly with coherent completion than with total throughput.

- Whether different collaboration styles require different completion strategies.
