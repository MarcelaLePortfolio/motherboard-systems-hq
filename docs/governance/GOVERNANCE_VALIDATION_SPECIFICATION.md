
# Governance Validation Specification

## Status

DRAFT

This document captures reconciled Governance Validation findings and serves as the authoritative foundation for future refinement.

## Identity

Governance Validation is the Organizational Readiness Analysis Layer.

## Core Question

"Is this Package fully operational as-is within current organizational reality?"

## Inputs

- Package

- Delegation

- Atlas Intelligence

- Organizational Reality

## Outputs

### Governance Findings

### Operational Requirements

### Capability Requirements

### Escalations

## Authority

May:

- Analyze

- Derive

- Escalate

- Consult organizational evidence

May Not:

- Modify meaning

- Create intent

- Assign ownership

- Route work

- Execute work

## Envelope Creation Rule

Envelope creation is prohibited until Governance Validation successfully completes.

## Escalation Model

All escalations return to Matilda.

Governance Validation does not resolve escalations.

## Relationship To Atlas

Atlas owns organizational intelligence.

Governance Validation consumes organizational intelligence.

## Relationship To Ellis

Governance Validation determines requirements.

Ellis determines ownership.

## Persistence

Governance Validation findings are persistent governance records.

## Organizational Visibility

Governance Validation may consult organizational reality and derives conclusions from evidence rather than assumed organizational state.
