
# Candidate HQ and Commercial Architecture

Status: CANDIDATE

## Purpose

Investigate the long-term architectural relationship between Motherboard Systems HQ and the commercial Motherboard product.

This corridor exists to preserve the emerging two-tier architecture without authorizing implementation.

---

## Candidate Architectural Principle

Motherboard Systems HQ serves as the internal organizational environment used to research, evolve, and govern the platform.

The commercial Motherboard product is a self-contained, customer-facing deployment derived from HQ while preserving appropriate architectural, governance, and intellectual property boundaries.

---

## Candidate Investigation Questions

- Which organizational capabilities belong exclusively to HQ?

- Which capabilities are required in every commercial deployment?

- Which governance artifacts are internal-only?

- Which architectural knowledge may be promoted into the commercial product?

- How should future product generations inherit improvements from HQ?
