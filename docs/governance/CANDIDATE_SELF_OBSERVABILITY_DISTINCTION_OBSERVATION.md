
# Self-Observability Distinction Observation

Status: PRESERVED OBSERVATION

## Context

During discussion of Atlas topology awareness, an interpretation drift occurred.

The original question concerned whether Atlas required explicit routing to Atlas-related artifacts.

The discussion temporarily shifted toward whether Atlas should actively investigate itself.

This distinction ultimately led to opening the Observability Scope Corridor.

## Observation

Self-observability and self-investigation may represent distinct concepts.

## Candidate Distinction

Self-Observability:

- Visibility into self-related artifacts.

- Access to self-related topology.

- Access to self-related lineage.

- Awareness of self-related organizational records.

Self-Investigation:

- Active inquiry regarding the self.

- Dedicated investigation of self-related questions.

- Ongoing analytical focus on self-related constructs.

- Prioritized evaluation of self-related observations.

## Evidence-Constrained Interpretation

Current evidence supports preservation of the distinction.

Current evidence does not establish:

- A formal self-observability ontology.

- A formal self-investigation ontology.

- Architectural requirements for either construct.

- Runtime behavior requirements.
