
# Corridor Reconciliation — Meaning Continuity Investigation

Status: RECONCILED

Origin: Meaning Continuity Investigation

## Reconciled Finding

Organizing Identity is the primary continuity construct governing interpretation lineage.

An interpretation may evolve substantially in:

- structure

- implementation

- presentation

- terminology

- capabilities

- supporting components

while preserving lineage, provided its organizing identity remains intact.

Lineage continuity is not primarily determined by:

- visibility

- structural persistence

- participation

- recognizability

- explainability

These constructs are better understood as indicators used to evaluate whether organizing identity has been preserved.

## Supporting Findings

### Visibility Is Not Sufficient

Historical elements may remain visible while no longer contributing meaningfully to the current interpretation.

Visibility alone does not establish lineage continuity.

### Participation Is Not Sufficient

Elements may continue participating within a system while serving a different organizing identity.

Participation alone does not establish lineage continuity.

### Structural Persistence Is Not Required

Prior structures may disappear entirely while remaining highly relevant to explaining the current interpretation.

Persistence alone is not required for continuity.

### Structural Relevance Is Not Sufficient

Structural relevance contributes evidence for continuity but does not independently determine continuity.

### Recognizability Is Evidence Rather Than Root Cause

Recognizability may indicate preservation of organizing identity.

Recognizability is not itself the continuity construct.

## Reconciled Interpretation

Continuity through change is best evaluated by determining whether the organizing identity of the interpretation remains intact.

When organizing identity remains intact:

- refinement may occur

- structures may evolve

- implementations may change

- presentation may change

while lineage remains preserved.

When organizing identity changes sufficiently that the interpretation no longer fulfills the same fundamental role within the governance system, lineage may cease and a new interpretive lineage may be required.

## Investigation Outcome

Current evidence supports reconciliation of Organizing Identity as the strongest known continuity construct.

Future contradictory evidence may reopen the corridor.

Reconciliation reflects stability rather than proof.
