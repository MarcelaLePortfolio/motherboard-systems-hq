
# V2 Reorientation Overhead Hypothesis

Status: CANDIDATE HYPOTHESIS

## Context

Repeated observations during Matilda Collaboration Mode V2 suggest that a meaningful portion of corridor activity may be devoted to:

- Reorientation.

- Context recap.

- Corridor topology recap.

- Future-path forecasting.

These behaviors may be valuable when context is unstable, participants are re-entering a corridor, or multiple interpretations require active management.

The hypothesis concerns situations where shared context appears stable and participants are already operating within an active investigative corridor.

## Candidate Hypothesis

When shared context is sufficiently stable, reducing reorientation, recap, and future-path forecasting may improve collaborative efficiency without reducing:

- Reasoning quality.

- Scrutiny.

- User-led correction.

- Reconciliation integrity.

## Candidate Mechanism

The potential benefit is not shorter responses.

The potential benefit is reduction of translation burden between reasoning steps.

Candidate areas of reduction include:

- Repeated corridor summaries.

- Repeated lineage reconstruction.

- Repeated topology recaps.

- Forecasting of multiple future directions before pressure-testing the current direction.

## Candidate Question

Does reducing reorientation overhead during active corridor investigation improve information density and investigation efficiency while preserving scrutiny, correction capacity, and reconciliation integrity?

## Evidence-Constrained Interpretation

Current evidence supports preservation of the hypothesis.

Current evidence does not establish:

- Improved collaboration quality.

- Improved reconciliation speed.

- Improved user comprehension.

- Improved discovery generation.

- Improved corridor outcomes.

No V2 behavior modifications have been validated.

## Relationship To Existing V2 Observations

Potentially related to:

- Reduced scrolling.

- Reduced repetition.

- Increased information density.

- Reduced constituent attention consumption.

- Preservation of scrutiny.
