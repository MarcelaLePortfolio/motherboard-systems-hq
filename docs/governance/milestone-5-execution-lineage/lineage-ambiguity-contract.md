Governance only.

---

## Purpose

The Lineage Ambiguity Contract defines:

- lineage ambiguity

- ambiguity artifacts

- ambiguity resolution

- escalation boundaries

- authoritative relationship separation

This contract governs unresolved lineage questions.

---

## Foundational Principle

Lineage ambiguity exists when Atlas cannot establish an authoritative relationship using available evidence.

Ambiguity is not failure.

Ambiguity is evidence that authoritative lineage requirements have not yet been satisfied.

---

## Ambiguity Determination Rule

A lineage ambiguity exists when:

- a relationship appears possible

- authoritative evidence is insufficient

- authoritative admission requirements are not satisfied

Potential relationships shall not enter the authoritative lineage graph.

---

## Authoritative Separation Rule

Authoritative relationships and lineage ambiguities are distinct categories.

Authoritative relationships:

- evidence-backed

- admitted to the lineage graph

Lineage ambiguities:

- unresolved

- evidence-insufficient

- excluded from authoritative lineage

---

## Ambiguity Artifact Rule

Lineage ambiguities may be preserved as ambiguity artifacts.

Ambiguity artifacts are historical records.

Ambiguity artifacts are not authoritative relationships.

---

## Direct Authority Resolution Rule

Atlas shall attempt resolution through the authority responsible for the affected artifact.

Examples:

Execution Artifact

→ Cade

Interpretation Artifact

→ Matilda

Validator Artifact

→ Validator Authority

Operational Artifact

→ Effie

Roadmap Artifact

→ Atlas

---

## Resolution Priority Rule

Atlas shall attempt resolution in the following order:

1. metadata review

2. evidence review

3. authority clarification

4. reconciliation review

5. payload-access procedures

The least invasive resolution path should be preferred.

---

## User Escalation Rule

User escalation is reserved for:

- intent authority issues

- approval authority issues

- path-critical business direction

- unresolved authoritative disagreement

Routine lineage ambiguities should not require user intervention.

---

## Payload Access Compatibility Rule

Lineage ambiguity alone does not authorize payload access.

Payload-access requirements remain governed by the Atlas Payload Access Contract.

---

## Resolution Outcomes

Lineage ambiguity may resolve into:

### Authoritative Relationship

Evidence requirements satisfied.

Relationship admitted.

---

### Clarified Non-Relationship

Evidence demonstrates that no relationship exists.

No relationship admitted.

---

### Persisting Ambiguity

Evidence remains insufficient.

Ambiguity artifact remains preserved.

---

## Historical Preservation Rule

Ambiguity artifacts are historical records.

Resolution does not erase ambiguity history.

Historical ambiguity may remain inspectable after resolution.

---

## Future Preservation Clause

Nothing in this contract shall prohibit future:

- event-level ambiguity handling

- expanded ambiguity categories

- lineage intelligence research

provided authority boundaries, evidence requirements, graph integrity requirements, and privacy protections remain preserved.
