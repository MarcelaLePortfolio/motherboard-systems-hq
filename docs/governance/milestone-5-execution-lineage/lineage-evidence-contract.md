## Purpose

The Lineage Evidence Contract defines:

- authoritative lineage evidence

- edge authorization requirements

- ambiguity determination

- evidence traceability requirements

This contract governs how lineage relationships become authoritative.

---

## Foundational Principle

Lineage relationships derive authority from evidence.

Atlas records relationships.

Evidence authorizes relationships.

Authority does not originate from Atlas itself.

---

## Evidence First Rule

Evidence outranks:

- inference

- probability

- speculation

- pattern matching

- confidence scores

Evidence is required for authoritative lineage admission.

---

## Evidence Sufficiency Rule

A lineage relationship may become authoritative only when sufficient evidence exists to support the relationship claim.

Relationship creation requires:

- identifiable source artifacts

- identifiable target artifacts

- evidence linking those artifacts

Missing evidence prevents relationship admission.

---

## Insufficient Evidence Rule

The following conditions constitute insufficient evidence:

- temporal proximity alone

- confidence scores alone

- probability estimates alone

- pattern matching alone

- inferred causality alone

These conditions may indicate ambiguity but may not authorize lineage relationships.

---

## Ambiguity Determination Rule

When evidence is insufficient:

- authoritative relationship admission is denied

- lineage ambiguity may be recorded

- ambiguity resolution procedures may begin

Insufficient evidence shall never be upgraded to authoritative lineage.

---

## Evidence Traceability Rule

Every authoritative relationship shall remain traceable to supporting evidence.

Evidence provenance must remain historically inspectable.

Relationship validity depends upon traceable evidence.

---

## Evidence Preservation Rule

Evidence supporting authoritative lineage relationships shall remain historically available.

Evidence may be archived.

Evidence may not be destroyed in a manner that invalidates authoritative lineage traceability.

---

## Lineage Validation Model

Lineage validation occurs at edge admission.

Relationship Claim

+

Supporting Evidence

=

Authoritative Relationship

Relationship Claim

+

Insufficient Evidence

=

Lineage Ambiguity

No separate lineage validator authority is required.

---

## Future Preservation Clause

Nothing in this contract shall prohibit future:

- event-level evidence models

- expanded evidence categories

- lineage intelligence research

provided evidence-first discipline, authority boundaries, privacy protections, and graph integrity requirements remain intact.
