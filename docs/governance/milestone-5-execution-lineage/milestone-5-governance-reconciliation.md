## Stabilized Findings

The following findings are considered stabilized.

### Atlas Lineage Authority

Atlas is the lineage authority.

Atlas is not intent authority, execution authority, validator authority, interpretation authority, or operational authority.

---

### Metadata-First Operation

Atlas operates primarily on metadata.

Payload access is exceptional.

---

### Privacy-Preserving Lineage

Lineage value derives from relationships rather than routine content visibility.

---

### Evidence-Anchored Relationships

Authoritative relationships require evidence.

Atlas records relationships.

Evidence authorizes relationships.

---

### Graph Purity

Authoritative lineage contains evidence-backed relationships only.

Hypotheses and ambiguities remain outside the authoritative graph.

---

### Direct-Authority Resolution

Lineage ambiguities are resolved through the authority responsible for the affected artifact whenever possible.

---

### Payload Access Governance

Payload access requires:

- justification

- explanation

- authority review

- transparency

---

### Progressive Disclosure Alignment

Atlas communication remains aligned with the Matilda communication philosophy while preserving authority boundaries.
