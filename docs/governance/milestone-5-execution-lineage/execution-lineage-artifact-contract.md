## Purpose

The Execution Lineage Artifact Contract defines:

- lineage artifact eligibility

- node participation rules

- lineage admission requirements

- artifact lifecycle states

- historical preservation requirements

This contract governs lineage nodes.

Relationship governance is defined separately.

---

## Foundational Principle

The execution-lineage graph exists to preserve consequential project history.

The graph is not intended to preserve:

- conversations

- telemetry

- logs

- observations

- runtime noise

The graph preserves decision-bearing artifacts capable of influencing future project paths.

---

## Lineage Artifact Definition

A Lineage Artifact is a durable, decision-bearing artifact capable of affecting:

- execution paths

- governance paths

- recovery paths

- operational paths

- roadmap paths

- milestone paths

Artifact existence alone does not qualify an artifact for lineage participation.

Future-path influence is required.

---

## Admission Rule

### Decision-Bearing Artifact Rule

An artifact may participate in the lineage graph only if its existence can alter:

- execution lineage

- governance lineage

- recovery lineage

- operational lineage

- roadmap lineage

- milestone lineage

Observational artifacts do not qualify.

## Lifecycle States

### Active

Artifact currently influences project-path decisions.

---

### Resolved

Artifact completed successfully.

Historical value remains.

---

### Superseded

Artifact replaced by a newer authoritative artifact.

Historical value remains.

---

### Archived

Artifact preserved for historical continuity.

No longer participates in active navigation.

---

## Lifecycle Constraints

Allowed transitions:

Active → Resolved

Active → Superseded

Resolved → Archived

Superseded → Archived

Lifecycle transitions do not remove artifacts from lineage history.

---

## Historical Preservation Rule

Lineage artifacts are permanent historical records.

Artifacts may:

- change lifecycle state

- become superseded

- become archived

Artifacts may not:

- be rewritten

- be deleted

- be historically erased

Historical continuity is preserved through append-only evolution.

---

## Privacy-Preserving Participation Principle

Lineage participation does not require payload preservation.

The lineage graph prioritizes:

- relationships

- classifications

- ownership

- lineage continuity

over:

- content retention

- payload retention

- user-data retention

Artifact participation does not imply payload visibility.

---

## Event-Level Preservation Clause

Current governance operates at the artifact level.

Nothing in this contract shall prohibit future event-level lineage expansion provided existing authority boundaries, privacy constraints, evidence requirements, and governance protections remain intact.
