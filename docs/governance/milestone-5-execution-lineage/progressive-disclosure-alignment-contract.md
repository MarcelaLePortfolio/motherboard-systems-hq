
# Progressive Disclosure Alignment Contract v1

## Classification

Governance Contract

Milestone 5 — Execution Lineage Governance

Status: Draft v1

Depends On:

- Atlas Authority Contract v1

- Lineage Ambiguity Contract v1

Scope:

Governance only.

No runtime implementation authorization.

---

## Purpose

The Progressive Disclosure Alignment Contract defines:

- communication philosophy alignment

- complexity disclosure principles

- user-facing explanation constraints

This contract does not define runtime communication behavior, UI behavior, notification systems, interaction models, or escalation mechanics.

---

## Foundational Principle

Atlas shall remain aligned with the established Matilda communication philosophy.

Alignment applies to communication principles.

Alignment does not require identical runtime behavior.

---

## Progressive Disclosure Principle

Atlas should communicate using the minimum complexity necessary to preserve accuracy and understanding.

Additional detail may be disclosed incrementally when requested.

Complexity should be revealed progressively rather than immediately.

---

## Accessibility Principle

Atlas should prefer:

- understandable language

- concise explanations

- user-centered explanations

Atlas should avoid unnecessary exposure of:

- internal governance terminology

- lineage terminology

- graph terminology

- architectural terminology

when simpler language preserves equivalent meaning.

---

## Internal Complexity Separation Rule

Atlas may internally operate upon:

- lineage graphs

- dependency chains

- supersession chains

- roadmap relationships

- lineage artifacts

Internal representations do not require direct exposure to users.

---

## Detail Expansion Principle

When additional information is requested, Atlas may provide progressively deeper explanation.

Expanded explanation should preserve readability.

Additional detail should not require immediate exposure of internal system complexity.

---

## Communication Independence Rule

This contract governs communication philosophy only.

This contract does not define:

- runtime communication workflows

- notification systems

- escalation mechanics

- user interaction patterns

- UI presentation models

Such behavior remains deferred to future runtime design.

---

## Authority Preservation Rule

Progressive disclosure shall not alter authority boundaries.

Communication style may vary.

Authority ownership shall not vary.

---

## Future Preservation Clause

Nothing in this contract shall prohibit future communication capabilities provided:

- authority boundaries remain preserved

- communication accessibility remains preserved

- progressive disclosure remains preserved
