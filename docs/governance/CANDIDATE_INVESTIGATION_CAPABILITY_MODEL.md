# Candidate Investigation Capability Model

Status: CANDIDATE MODEL

Origin:

Organizational Investigation Corridor
August 2026

---

# Purpose

Capture the emerging architectural hypothesis that investigation represents a
cross-cutting organizational capability rather than the responsibility of a
single department or authority.

This document does not establish runtime behavior, ownership, governance
authority, implementation, or organizational restructuring.

No implementation is authorized.

---

# Investigation Background

This investigation reviewed recurring governance artifacts including:

- Candidate observations
- Candidate hypotheses
- Reconciliation documents
- Corridor artifacts
- Atlas organizational intelligence
- Matilda governance artifacts
- Governance Validation artifacts
- Cade execution audits

The investigation sought to determine whether investigation already possesses
an implicit organizational owner.

No evidence supporting exclusive ownership was identified.

Instead, investigation consistently appeared as a recurring organizational
activity spanning multiple authorities.

# Cross-Authority Participation

Current evidence indicates that investigations may involve multiple
organizational authorities.

Examples include:

Matilda

- interpretation
- collaboration
- architectural exploration

Atlas

- organizational intelligence
- lineage
- relationship cartography
- evidence preservation

Governance Validation

- governance boundary evaluation
- validation findings

Cade

- execution audits
- implementation-specific investigation

Current evidence does not support investigation belonging exclusively to any one
of these authorities.

---

# Relationship To Capability Routing

The existing organizational model routes work by capabilities rather than by
actors.

Current evidence suggests investigation naturally aligns with this pattern.

Conceptually:

Investigation Request

↓

Required Capabilities

↓

Capability Routing

↓

Participating Authorities

↓

Evidence-Based Findings

Current evidence supports compatibility with the existing Capability Routing
Model.

---

# Candidate Interpretation

Investigation may represent an organizational capability that is satisfied by
multiple authorities according to the evidence required by a particular
question.

This interpretation preserves existing authority boundaries while avoiding the
creation of a new department without supporting evidence.

---

# Does Not Establish

Current evidence does not establish:

- an Investigation Department
- Investigation ownership
- runtime implementation
- routing authority
- organizational restructuring
- new governance authority

---

# Open Questions

Future investigation should determine:

- Which capabilities are commonly required during investigations.
- Whether investigation requests should themselves become Packages.
- Whether Ellis should route investigation work.
- Whether Atlas should preserve investigation lineage separately.
- How investigation outcomes integrate with reconciliation workflows.
