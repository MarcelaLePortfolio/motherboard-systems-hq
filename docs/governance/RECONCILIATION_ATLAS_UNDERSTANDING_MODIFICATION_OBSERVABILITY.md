
# Atlas Understanding Modification Observability Reconciliation

Status: RECONCILED

## Origin

This reconciliation emerged from investigation of:

- Participation Frequency.

- Inspection / Evaluation Pipeline.

- Intervention Corridor.

The central question became:

What may Atlas legitimately infer from observable collaboration artifacts?

## Reconciled Finding

Atlas should infer understanding-modification state from observable artifacts rather than attempting to infer hidden cognitive processes.

## Observability Boundary

Atlas cannot directly observe:

- Thinking.

- Inspection.

- Evaluation.

- Realization.

- Internal participant activity.

These activities remain outside Atlas observability.

## Observable Artifacts

Atlas may observe:

- Continue artifacts.

- Corrective interventions.

- Expansive interventions.

- New corridors.

- Reconciliations.

- Understanding-modification artifacts.

## Understanding Modification Principle

The primary observable question is:

Did understanding change?

rather than:

What hidden activity occurred?

## Continue Interpretation

Current evidence supports the interpretation:

continue

        ↓

No corrective intervention observed

No expansive intervention observed

Understanding appears unchanged

Atlas is not required to determine whether inspection, evaluation, or thinking occurred internally.

## Intervention Interpretation

Current evidence supports interpretation of intervention as an observable understanding-modification event.

Examples may include:

- Corrections.

- Structural distinctions.

- Extensions.

- New branches.

- Reconciliation-producing observations.

## Atlas Inference Limits

Atlas should not infer:

- Absence of thinking.

- Absence of inspection.

- Absence of evaluation.

- Absence of participation.

from continue artifacts alone.

Atlas may only infer the absence of an observable understanding-modification event.

## Architectural Significance

This reconciliation shifts Atlas from:

Hidden Process Inference

toward:

Observable State Transition Tracking

The emphasis becomes:

Understanding Changed

or

Understanding Unchanged

based upon observable evidence.

## What This Reconciliation Establishes

This reconciliation establishes:

- Atlas observability should remain artifact-based.

- Atlas should track understanding-modification events.

- Atlas should not model hidden cognition as observable fact.

- Continue artifacts may indicate understanding stability.

## What This Reconciliation Does Not Establish

This reconciliation does not establish:

- A cognition model.

- An inspection model.

- An evaluation model.

- Atlas runtime requirements.

- Atlas implementation requirements.

- Atlas authority.
