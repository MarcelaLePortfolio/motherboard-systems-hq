
# Influence Versus Impact Distinction

Status: PRESERVED OBSERVATION

## Context

During investigation of participation frequency, influence emerged as a candidate construct.

Subsequent discussion suggested that influence and impact may not be equivalent.

## Candidate Distinction

Influence:

- Potential capacity to alter investigation state.

Impact:

- Observable alteration of investigation state.

## Examples

Potential impacts include:

- Discovery events.

- Structural reclassifications.

- Corridor bifurcations.

- Reconciliation events.

- Governance clarifications.

## Evidence-Constrained Interpretation

Current evidence supports investigation of whether impact functions as observable evidence that influence occurred.

The observation does not establish:

- A formal ontology for influence.

- A formal ontology for impact.

- That all influence produces visible impact.

- That all impact originates from influence.
