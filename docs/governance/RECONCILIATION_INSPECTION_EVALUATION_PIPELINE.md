
# Inspection Evaluation Pipeline Reconciliation

Status: RECONCILED

## Origin Question

The corridor originated from investigation of evaluation activity following reconciliation of the Participation Frequency Corridor.

A central question emerged:

Is evaluation a single construct, or does it contain multiple distinct activities?

## Reconciled Finding

Current evidence supports separation of:

Inspection

and

Evaluation

as distinct activities within the observed collaborative investigation environment.

## Inspection

Inspection appears directed toward the reasoning artifact itself.

Observed inspection activities include:

- Comprehension.

- Coherence.

- Contradiction detection.

Representative inspection questions:

- Do I understand this?

- Does it make sense?

- Can I contradict it?

## Evaluation

Evaluation appears directed toward the implications or value of the reasoning.

Observed evaluation activities include:

- Plausibility assessment.

- Generativity assessment.

Representative evaluation questions:

- Does it sound true?

- Does it generate insights?

## Candidate Pipeline

Observed behavior is most consistently explained by:

Reasoning Presented

        ↓

Inspection

        ↓

Evaluation

        ↓

PASS

        ↓

Continue

FAIL

        ↓

Intervention

## Continue Reinterpretation

The corridor produced a revised interpretation of:

"continue"

Within the observed environment, "continue" may represent:

Inspection:

PASS

Evaluation:

PASS

Action:

CONTINUE

The corridor found no evidence that "continue" necessarily implies absence of participation.

## Intervention Reinterpretation

Current evidence supports interpretation of intervention as a response to detected insufficiency.

Potential intervention triggers include:

Inspection Failures:

- Comprehension failure.

- Coherence failure.

- Contradiction detection.

Evaluation Failures:

- Plausibility concerns.

- Generativity gaps.

- Explanatory insufficiencies.

## What This Reconciliation Establishes

This reconciliation establishes:

- Inspection and evaluation should not be treated as equivalent constructs.

- The observed inspection activities differ from the observed evaluation activities.

- "Continue" may represent successful passage through both activities.

- Interventions may emerge when inspection or evaluation identifies a failure, insufficiency, contradiction, or gap.

## What This Reconciliation Does Not Establish

This reconciliation does not establish:

- A model of human cognition.

- A model of human reasoning.

- A universal inspection pipeline.

- A universal evaluation pipeline.

- That all interventions originate from failure conditions.

- Participant-independent applicability.

## Scope

This reconciliation applies only to observations arising within the current collaborative investigation environment.

## Successor Questions

Open questions include:

- Whether the inspection hierarchy is stable.

- Whether the hierarchy is participant-specific.

- Whether additional evaluation activities exist.

- Whether intervention always represents failure or insufficiency.

- How Atlas should observe inspection and evaluation activity.
