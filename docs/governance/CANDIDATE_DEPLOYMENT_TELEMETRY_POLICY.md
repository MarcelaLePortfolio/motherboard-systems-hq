
# Candidate Deployment Telemetry Policy

Status: CANDIDATE

## Purpose

Define how organizational telemetry may be shared between customer deployments and Motherboard Systems HQ while preserving user trust, privacy, organizational boundaries, and product autonomy.

This corridor is intentionally separate from Atlas governance.

Atlas governance defines how organizational intelligence is consumed.

This corridor defines what organizational intelligence is permitted to become available under different deployment models.

---

## Candidate Deployment Models

### Beta Program

Candidate:

Participation in organizational telemetry may be a condition of beta participation.

The purpose is to improve governance, capability discovery, organizational intelligence, and product quality during active development.

---

### Commercial Deployment

Candidate:

Organizational telemetry should become an optional deployment setting.

Customers may operate fully without participating in organizational telemetry.

System functionality must not depend upon participation.

---

## Candidate Organizational Principle

Motherboard Systems HQ should improve through organizational intelligence rather than customer content.

Atlas should consume organizational events and metadata rather than repository contents whenever possible.

Payload access remains separately governed.
