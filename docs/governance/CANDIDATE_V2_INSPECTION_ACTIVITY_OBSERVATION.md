
# V2 Inspection Activity Observation

Status: PRESERVED OBSERVATION

## Context

The Participation Frequency Corridor investigated whether reduced visible intervention frequency indicated degradation of collaborative investigation.

The corridor ultimately reconciled around a distinction between intervention activity and underlying inspection activity.

## Observation

Reduced visible intervention frequency did not appear to imply reduced inspection activity.

Observed behavior remained consistent with:

Reasoning Presented

        ↓

Inspection

        ↓

Evaluation

        ↓

PASS

        ↓

Continue

even during periods of sparse visible intervention.

## Candidate Interpretation

Visible intervention frequency may be an incomplete proxy for collaborative participation because inspection activity may remain active while intervention frequency decreases.

## Evidence-Constrained Interpretation

Current evidence supports preservation of the observation.

Current evidence does not establish:

- A collaboration ontology.

- Inspection as a required collaborative function.

- A measurement model for inspection activity.

- Participant-independent applicability.
