
# Governance Validation Charter

## Purpose

Governance Validation exists to determine whether delegated meaning can safely become operational work.

Governance Validation does not determine intent, assign actors, perform engineering planning, or execute work.

Governance Validation reviews delegated Packages and determines whether operationalization may proceed.

---

# Mission

Ensure delegated meaning can safely become operational work without requiring:

- Unauthorized assumption

- Scope expansion

- Governance violation

- Authority escalation

- Unresolved ambiguity

- Contradiction

- Operational invention

---

# Inputs

Governance Validation receives:

- Delegated Package

- Delegation Authorization

- Applicable Governance Rules

- Applicable Organizational Constraints

---

# Outputs

Governance Validation produces:

## Validation Status

One of:

- PASS

- RESOLUTION REQUIRED

---

## Governance Findings

Documented findings generated during validation.

Examples:

- Ambiguity Findings

- Scope Findings

- Containment Findings

- Constraint Findings

- Conflict Findings

---

## Required Capabilities

Capabilities required to operationalize the Package.

Examples:

- engineering_planning

- repository_analysis

- desktop_operations

- external_backup

Governance Validation derives capabilities.

Governance Validation does not assign actors.

---

## Operational Corridor

Operational corridor authorized for downstream work.

Examples:

- planning_only

- desktop_operations

- execution_authorized

Operational corridors define permitted operational boundaries.

---

# Validation Categories

## Interpretation Fidelity

Question:

Does the Package accurately and consistently represent the delegated intent?

Checks:

- Internal consistency

- Contradiction detection

- Reference integrity

---

## Scope Fidelity

Question:

Can the work be understood without expanding scope?

Checks:

- Scope clarity

- Scope completeness

- Scope containment

---

## Containment Fidelity

Question:

Are protected boundaries clearly identified?

Checks:

- Allowed modification areas

- Excluded modification areas

- Protected systems

---

## Constraint Fidelity

Question:

Are required constraints clearly represented?

Checks:

- Governance constraints

- Architectural constraints

- Operational constraints

---

## Resolution Status

Question:

Are all known governance concerns resolved?

Checks:

- Open findings

- Unresolved conflicts

- Unresolved ambiguity

If unresolved concerns remain:

Validation Status = RESOLUTION REQUIRED

---

## Operational Completeness

Question:

Can operational actors perform work without inventing meaning?

Checks:

- Intent completeness

- Scope completeness

- Constraint completeness

- Dependency completeness

If operational invention would be required:

Validation Status = RESOLUTION REQUIRED

---

## Capability Determinability

Question:

Can required capabilities be determined?

Checks:

- Capability identification

- Capability completeness

Required capabilities must be derivable before operationalization.

---

# PASS Criteria

Governance Validation may issue PASS only when:

- Interpretation fidelity is sufficient

- Scope fidelity is sufficient

- Containment fidelity is sufficient

- Constraint fidelity is sufficient

- Resolution status is clear

- Operational completeness is sufficient

- Required capabilities can be derived

---

# Resolution Required Criteria

Governance Validation must issue RESOLUTION REQUIRED when:

- Meaning cannot be safely operationalized

- Scope is unclear

- Constraints are unclear

- Containment is unclear

- Operational invention would be required

- Required capabilities cannot be determined

- Governance conflicts remain unresolved

---

# Non-Responsibilities

Governance Validation does not own:

- Intent Interpretation

- Actor Assignment

- Capability Registry Ownership

- Engineering Planning

- Desktop Operations

- Execution

- Organizational Intelligence
