
# Headquarters Organizational Charter

## Purpose

This document defines the canonical organizational structure of the Motherboard Headquarters operating model.

The headquarters is organized around authority domains rather than implementation details. Each department owns a distinct responsibility and may not assume authority belonging to another department.

# Organizational Principle

Authority domains must remain separated.

No department may assume authority belonging to another department.

Packages own meaning.

Governance Validation owns operationalization review.

Ellis owns assignment.

Departments own capabilities.

Atlas owns organizational intelligence and relationship intelligence.
