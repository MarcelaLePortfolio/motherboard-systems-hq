## 2. Approved Governance Decisions

### G1 — B-01 Authority Scope

APPROVED CONDITIONALLY.

The existing Ellis accountable administrator may become eligible for bounded B-01 registration approval through separately established, independently authorized, operation-specific delegation.

Existing department credential administration authority does not automatically extend to B-01.

No B-01 registration authority is operationally established by this approval.

### G2 — Minimum Participant Model

APPROVED CONDITIONALLY.

Two distinct human participants are the minimum permitted configuration when all independence, eligibility, custody, and review requirements are satisfied.

The proposed allocation is:

- Participant 1: Accountable governance administrator and independently authorized registration approver.
- Participant 2: Physical iPad operator, signing-key controller, registration applicant, and evidence submitter.

This approval does not appoint either participant to B-01 or B-03 operations.

### G3 — Conditional Registration/Acceptance Role Overlap

APPROVED CONDITIONALLY.

The B-01 registration approver may also serve as the B-03 final acceptance authority only when:

1. Both permissions are separately and legitimately authorized.
2. The approver did not create or exclusively control the evidence.
3. B-02 physical-origin corroboration is independently established.
4. Acceptance does not require an independent audit of the approver's own registration conduct.
5. Cryptographic, lifecycle, revocation, evidence-integrity, and conflict checks pass.
6. The applicable acceptance procedure permits this combination.

No final acceptance authority is activated by this policy.

### G4 — Mandatory Independent-Review Exception

APPROVED.

A separate independently authorized reviewer is required whenever final acceptance must independently audit the registration approver's own conduct or a prohibited conflict prevents independent review.

Changing role labels, adding software agents, or relying exclusively on cryptographic verification cannot substitute for genuine independence.

### G5 — Mandatory Operational Activation Gate

ADOPTED — UNSATISFIED.

Operational activation is prohibited until the following are independently established and verified:

- Legitimate B-01 registration authority and operation-specific delegation.
- Independently controlled registration approval and trusted-key publication.
- Verified signing-key ownership, custody, and registration integrity.
- Human participant eligibility and conflict-of-interest compliance.
- Session authorization, expiration, closure, and revocation enforcement.
- Authoritative acceptance/revocation ordering.
- Trusted cryptographic-result and evidence binding.
- Complete bypass-prevention coverage.
- Ephemeral signing-key feasibility and timing.
- Independently corroborated B-02 physical-device association.
- Approved B-03 evidence-review and final-acceptance procedures.

G5 cannot be satisfied merely by this governance approval.

## 3. Separation-of-Duties Invariants

- No self-approval of signing-key registration.

- No self-appointment of independent reviewers.
- No independent acceptance of one's own uncorroborated evidence.
- No independent audit performed by the subject of that audit.
- No cryptographic signature treated as sufficient proof of physical origin.
- No technical verifier granting governance authority.
- No acceptance after failed, unavailable, expired, or revoked prerequisites.
- No retroactive authorization.
- No production issuance or broader execution authority.
