
# Candidate Finding: Understanding As Objective Distinction

Status: CANDIDATE

Owner: Atlas

Classification: Collaboration Architecture Investigation

## Origin

This finding emerged during investigation of:

- Matilda Collaboration Mode lineage

- Candidate V3 attribution

- Collaboration Architecture Framework

- Accessibility / Reasoning / Governance decomposition

The corridor progressively separated several classes of constructs that had previously been discussed together.

---

## Candidate Distinction

The investigation increasingly suggested that the following categories may be distinct:

### Mechanisms

Examples:

- Natural-language restatement

- Contextual anchors

- Compression

- Reduced scrolling

- Reduced repetition

Mechanisms appear to influence how collaboration occurs.

---

### Functions

Examples:

- Accessibility

- Reasoning

- Governance

Functions appear to describe major forms of collaborative activity.

---

### Outcomes

Examples:

- Discovery

- Distinction formation

- Reconciliation

Outcomes appear to result from collaborative activity.

---

### Objectives

Candidate Example:

- Improved understanding

Objectives appear to describe why mechanisms, functions, and outcomes are valuable.

---

## Observations

During the investigation:

- Natural language appeared valuable because it improved access to reasoning.

- Reasoning appeared valuable because it improved understanding of active questions.

- Discovery appeared valuable because it improved understanding.

- Governance appeared valuable because it preserved understanding produced during investigation.

---

## Candidate Assessment

Current evidence suggests that several constructs previously discussed as peers may actually belong to different categories.

Examples:

- Natural language may be a mechanism.

- Accessibility may be a function.

- Discovery may be an outcome.

- Understanding may be an objective.

---

## Constraints

This finding is not reconciled.

The investigation does not establish that understanding is the root construct.

The investigation only establishes that understanding currently appears to explain the value of multiple previously identified mechanisms, functions, and outcomes more coherently than treating those constructs as equivalent.

Additional pressure testing is required.
