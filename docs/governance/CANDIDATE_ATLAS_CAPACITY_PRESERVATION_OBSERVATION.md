## Context

- Collaboration Architecture.

- Evaluation.

- Observability Separation.

A recurring pattern emerged.

As investigation sophistication increased, participants increasingly performed:

- Lineage preservation.

- Metadata classification.

- Topology tracking.

- Impact tracking.

- Influence tracking.

- Corridor mapping.

These activities appeared valuable but also appeared to consume investigative capacity.

## Observation

Observability burden may grow alongside investigation sophistication.
