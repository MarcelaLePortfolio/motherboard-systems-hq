
# Atlas Organizational Intelligence Ledger

Status: ACTIVE

Owner: Atlas

Purpose:

Capture cross-corridor observations, emerging organizational patterns, recurring correlations, investigation meta-findings, and candidate organizational heuristics.

This ledger exists to preserve observations that do not clearly belong to:

- Governance Doctrine

- Reconciliation Findings

- Meaning Continuity

- Execution Ontology

- Matilda Collaboration Mode

- Individual corridor artifacts

The ledger is observational.

Entries remain observations or hypotheses until sufficient evidence exists for promotion into a reconciled finding or governance artifact.

---

## Entry Classification

### Observation

A directly observed pattern.

No causal explanation required.

---

### Hypothesis

A proposed explanation for one or more observations.

Requires additional evidence.

---

### Candidate Finding

A pattern showing repeated support but not yet reconciled.

---

### Reconciled Finding

A pattern that has satisfied reconciliation requirements and been accepted through governance review.

### Organizational Pattern Detection and Proposal Generation

Future Atlas runtimes should evaluate significant organizational events for potential cross-corridor observations, hypotheses, correlations, and investigation targets.

Examples of triggering events include:

- Corridor reconciliation

- Governance safeguard creation

- Governance incidents

- Recovery events

- Major user-led corrections

- Hypothesis reconciliation

- Investigation closure

Atlas may propose:

- Observation entries

- Hypothesis entries

- Candidate correlations

- Candidate investigation targets

Atlas may not:

- Reconcile findings

- Promote hypotheses into findings

- Modify governance doctrine

- Establish organizational truth

Atlas functions as an observational intelligence system.

Governance Validation, Matilda, and User Authority remain responsible for evaluation, approval, and promotion.

### Purpose

Prevent loss of cross-corridor organizational intelligence.

Preserve potentially significant patterns until sufficient evidence exists for validation or reconciliation.
