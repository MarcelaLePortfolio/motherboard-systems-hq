
# Evaluation Intervention Distinction

Status: PRESERVED OBSERVATION

## Context

The participation-frequency corridor initially focused on visible interventions.

Subsequent pressure-testing suggested that evaluation activity may continue even when visible interventions are infrequent.

## Observation

Evaluation and intervention may represent distinct activities.

A participant may:

- Evaluate.

- Scrutinize.

- Compare against existing models.

- Assess coherence.

without producing a visible intervention.

## Candidate Distinction

Evaluation:

- Internal activity.

- Scrutiny.

- Assessment.

- Comparison against existing understanding.

Intervention:

- Visible contribution.

- Correction.

- Challenge.

- Distinction.

- New realization.

## Evidence-Constrained Interpretation

Current evidence supports preservation of the distinction.

Current evidence does not establish:

- A collaboration ontology.

- Evaluation as a required collaborative function.

- Measurement methods for evaluation activity.
