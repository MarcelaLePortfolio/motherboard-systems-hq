
# Candidate Finding — Substantial Validity

Status: NOT RECONCILED

Origin: Meaning Continuity Investigation

## Candidate Finding

An interpretation may remain substantially valid when the current interpretation can naturally explain why the previous interpretation existed and appeared reasonable at the time.

Lineage continuity is preserved when the prior interpretation remains a legitimate and naturally understandable ancestor of the current interpretation.

Rebuild differences alone do not imply lineage break.

Learning and refinement may legitimately produce a different rebuild while preserving interpretation lineage.

## Natural-Language Test

If the current interpretation can naturally explain:

- why the previous interpretation existed

- what it was attempting to accomplish

- why it appeared reasonable at the time

- how the current interpretation emerged from it

without mental acrobatics, revisionism, or strained rationalization,

then substantial validity may still exist.

## Supporting Example

Operator Workspace Evolution

The later understanding did not render the earlier understanding unintelligible.

The current understanding can still explain:

- why the original workspace was designed as it was

- what problem it was solving

- why the design appeared reasonable given the information available at the time

This suggests refinement rather than replacement.

## Open Questions

- Is natural explainability sufficient for substantial validity?

- What evidence is sufficient to establish lineage break?

- Can substantial validity exist when major conclusions change?

- What organizational consequences follow from lineage continuation versus lineage break?

## Additional Investigation Notes

### Structural Relevance vs Residual Survival

Current investigation suggests that mere survival of prior interpretive elements may be insufficient for lineage continuity.

A prior interpretation may remain:

- visible

- remembered

- explainable

without remaining structurally relevant to the current interpretation.

Current candidate distinction:

Active Lineage:

- Prior interpretation continues to contribute meaningful explanatory or organizational structure to the current interpretation.

Residual Lineage:

- Prior interpretation remains visible or historically present but no longer contributes meaningful structure to the current interpretation.

### Candidate Observation

Mere survival is not equivalent to participation.

Historical remnants may persist after lineage has effectively weakened or ended.

### Candidate Question

Does lineage break occur when a new interpretation emerges?

Or does lineage break occur when the prior interpretation ceases to be structurally relevant to the current interpretation?

### Supporting Analogy

Room Evolution Example:

- Pink-centered room

- Pink room with Tiffany floral additions

- Tiffany-centered room with remaining pink remnants

- Removal of final pink lineage-bearing elements

Investigation suggests lineage weakens as prior elements become residual rather than active.

Lineage may end when prior elements cease to meaningfully participate in the identity of the current whole.
