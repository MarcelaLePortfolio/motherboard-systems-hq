
# Candidate Finding — Substantial Validity

Status: NOT RECONCILED

Origin: Meaning Continuity Investigation

## Candidate Finding

An interpretation may remain substantially valid when the current interpretation can naturally explain why the previous interpretation existed and appeared reasonable at the time.

Lineage continuity is preserved when the prior interpretation remains a legitimate and naturally understandable ancestor of the current interpretation.

Rebuild differences alone do not imply lineage break.

Learning and refinement may legitimately produce a different rebuild while preserving interpretation lineage.

## Natural-Language Test

If the current interpretation can naturally explain:

- why the previous interpretation existed

- what it was attempting to accomplish

- why it appeared reasonable at the time

- how the current interpretation emerged from it

without mental acrobatics, revisionism, or strained rationalization,

then substantial validity may still exist.

## Supporting Example

Operator Workspace Evolution

The later understanding did not render the earlier understanding unintelligible.

The current understanding can still explain:

- why the original workspace was designed as it was

- what problem it was solving

- why the design appeared reasonable given the information available at the time

This suggests refinement rather than replacement.
