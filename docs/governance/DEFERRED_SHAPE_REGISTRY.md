
# Deferred Shape Registry

## Purpose

The Deferred Shape Registry preserves implementation-shaped reasoning that survived architectural pressure-testing but has not been promoted to constitutional architecture, implementation scope, or active milestone status.

A deferred shape is not an authorization to implement.

A deferred shape records:

- why the shape was considered,

- what constitutional question produced it,

- why it was deferred,

- what future condition should cause re-evaluation.

Wake-up conditions do not trigger implementation automatically. They trigger architectural re-evaluation.

---

# DSR-001 — Department Participation Resolution

## Descriptor

Deferred departmental implementation shape for resolving internal participation after Ellis assigns accountability to a department.

## Origin Corridor

Governance Artifact Consumption → Ellis Departmental Ownership Boundary → Department Participation Contract

## Moment Label

Post-ASSIGNED Governance Artifact Consumption / Ellis-to-Department Boundary Clarification

## Architectural Context

The corridor clarified that Ellis assigns accountability to departments, not directly to individual agents or actors.

Departments are responsible for determining internal participation.

Individual actors do not automatically inherit departmental authority merely because they are currently the only member of a department.

## Stabilized Constitutional Boundary

Ellis assigns departments.

Departments determine internal participation.

Teams or actors perform implementation planning and execution within departmental authority.

## Hypothetical Shape

A department may eventually expose a participation-resolution mechanism that knows the department's internal members, capabilities, policies, and availability.

That mechanism may determine:

- lead participant,

- supporting participants,

- reviewers,

- required specialties,

- temporary working team composition.

## Status

Architecturally compatible.

Constitutionally optional.

Implementation deferred.

Not promoted to invariant.

Not implementation-authorized.

## Promotion Decision

Not promoted because multiple implementation shapes remain plausible and repository evidence does not yet justify selecting one.

The constitutional boundary was stabilized without requiring a department runtime, participation resolver, team-formation mechanism, or staffing algorithm.

## Wake-Up Condition

Re-open this deferred shape for architectural re-evaluation when any department, not only Engineering, meets one or more of the following conditions:

- a department contains more than one actor,

- a department must determine internal participation for an Ellis-assigned envelope,

- Ellis assigns accountability to a department and actor-level participation can no longer be safely inferred,

- departmental membership, capability, availability, or review policy must influence participation,

- a department needs to form a temporary working team.

## Expected First Consumer

Engineering Department is the expected first likely consumer because Cade is currently the only engineering actor and Engineering is the most likely near-term department to expand.

This is not an architectural dependency.

If another department expands first, this same deferred shape should wake up for that department instead.

## Non-Goals

This deferred shape does not authorize:

- new lifecycle states,

- Envelope contract expansion,

- scheduler integration,

- routing integration,

- worker integration,

- execution authority,

- autonomous departmental behavior,

- implementation of a Department Runtime,

- implementation of a Participation Resolver,

- implementation of team formation.

## Re-Entry Rule

When a wake-up condition is met, re-open this shape as an implementation-readiness or architecture corridor.

Do not automatically implement it.

First determine whether the original deferral rationale still holds.
