
# Phase 746 Execution Interruption Governance

## Status

Planning-only execution interruption governance architecture document.

This file does not implement execution authority.

## Purpose

Define the governance interruption architecture required before any future execution-capable proposal could claim bounded interruption survivability.

## Locked Principle

Interruption governance is not execution authorization.

A system may classify interruption survivability without gaining runtime mutation authority.

## Execution Interruption Governance Definition

Execution interruption governance refers to future governance pathways responsible for:

- classifying interruption survivability,

- classifying governance-preserving suspension behavior,

- classifying rollback interruption continuity,

- classifying reconciliation interruption continuity,

- classifying escalation interruption continuity,

- classifying quarantine interruption continuity,

- classifying containment interruption continuity,

- and classifying continuity-preserving pause behavior

without activating execution authority.

## Allowed Interruption Governance Activities

Future interruption governance systems may:

- validate interruption survivability continuity,

- validate rollback/reconciliation interruption linkage,

- validate escalation interruption continuity,

- validate governance persistence survivability,

- validate lineage survivability under interruption,

- validate quarantine survivability continuity,

- validate containment survivability continuity,

- validate continuity certification survivability,

- generate non-authoritative interruption governance reports,

- escalate governance concerns.

## Explicitly Forbidden Interruption Governance Activities

Interruption governance systems must NOT:

- mutate runtime state,

- authorize execution,

- invoke rollback execution,

- invoke reconciliation mutation,

- bypass governance review,

- self-authorize orchestration,

- promote sandbox state into production,

- suppress escalation states,

- downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- infer legitimacy from interruption recovery alone,

- bypass prototype review corridors,

- bypass certification governance,

- bypass readiness attestation governance,

- bypass trust stratification governance,

- bypass containment governance,

- or bypass continuity certification governance.

## Required Interruption Governance States

Future interruption governance systems must eventually support:

### 1. INTERRUPTED

Meaning:

- bounded interruption detected,

- governance continuity preserved,

- execution authority still absent.

## 2. PAUSED_FOR_REVIEW

Meaning:

- governance review required before continuation.

## 3. QUARANTINED

Meaning:

- interruption isolated,

- governance continuity preserved,

- investigation required.

## 4. ESCALATED_FOR_REVIEW

Meaning:

- interruption concerns require higher governance review classification.

## 5. INTERRUPTION_CONTAINED

Meaning:

- containment integrity preserved,

- governance continuity maintained.

## 6. INVALID_INTERRUPTION_STATE

Meaning:

- governance continuity compromised,

- interruption survivability unsafe,

- execution eligibility revoked.

## Mandatory INVALID Conditions

Interruption governance becomes INVALID automatically if:

- interruption pathways authorize execution,

- interruption pathways bypass governance review,

- interruption pathways mutate production state,

- interruption pathways suppress escalation states,

- interruption pathways bypass rollback requirements,

- interruption pathways bypass reconciliation requirements,

- interruption pathways downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- interruption pathways bypass prototype review corridors,

- interruption pathways bypass certification governance,

- interruption pathways bypass readiness attestation governance,

- interruption pathways bypass trust stratification governance,

- interruption pathways bypass containment governance,

- interruption pathways bypass continuity certification governance,

- or interruption pathways become orchestration-capable.

- audit traceability,

- runtime isolation guarantees,

- target classifications,

## Carry-Forward Invariants

- Preview remains read-only.

- Renderer remains non-authoritative.

- Sandbox remains isolated.

- Matilda remains governance-only validation.

- Governance remains higher authority than execution eligibility.

- No mutation occurs without future governed implementation.

## Phase 746 Limitation

Phase 746 may define interruption governance architecture only.

No execution runtime, orchestration engine, mutation-capable interruption system, or production execution bridge may be implemented.

## Locked Conclusion

Future execution interruption governance systems must remain permanently separated from mutation-capable execution authority so interruption review, continuity review, containment review, trust review, certification review, readiness review, rollback review, reconciliation review, governance review, and execution eligibility review cannot silently evolve into uncontrolled orchestration infrastructure.
