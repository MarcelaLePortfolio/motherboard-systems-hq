
# Phase 746 Execution Containment Doctrine

## Status

Planning-only execution containment doctrine architecture document.

This file does not implement execution authority.

## Purpose

Define the governance containment doctrine required before any future execution-capable proposal could claim bounded containment integrity.

## Locked Principle

Containment is not execution authorization.

A system may define containment doctrine without gaining runtime mutation authority.

## Execution Containment Definition

Execution containment doctrine refers to future governance pathways responsible for:

- defining bounded failure containment,

- defining quarantine continuity,

- defining interruption isolation,

- defining rollback containment integrity,

- defining reconciliation containment integrity,

- defining governance-preserving interruption handling,

- defining trust-preserving quarantine pathways,

- and defining escalation-preserving containment continuity

without activating execution authority.

## Allowed Containment Activities

Future containment systems may:

- classify containment integrity,

- validate quarantine continuity,

- validate rollback containment linkage,

- validate reconciliation containment linkage,

- validate escalation continuity under failure,

- validate governance persistence continuity,

- validate lineage continuity under interruption,

- validate runtime isolation guarantees,

- generate non-authoritative containment reports,

- escalate governance concerns.

## Explicitly Forbidden Containment Activities

Containment systems must NOT:

- mutate runtime state,

- authorize execution,

- invoke rollback execution,

- invoke reconciliation mutation,

- bypass governance review,

- self-authorize orchestration,

- promote sandbox state into production,

- suppress escalation states,

- downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- infer legitimacy from interruption survival,

- bypass prototype review corridors,

- bypass certification governance,

- bypass readiness attestation governance,

- or bypass trust stratification governance.

## Required Containment States

Future containment systems must eventually support:

### 1. CONTAINED

Meaning:

- bounded governance continuity preserved,

- containment integrity maintained,

- execution authority still absent.

## 2. QUARANTINED

Meaning:

- interruption isolated,

- governance continuity preserved,

- investigation required.

## 3. ESCALATED_FOR_REVIEW

Meaning:

- governance concerns require higher containment review classification.

## 4. CONTAINMENT_BREACH

Meaning:

- governance boundaries compromised,

- containment integrity violated,

- execution eligibility revoked.

## 5. INVALID_CONTAINMENT_STATE

Meaning:

- governance continuity irrecoverable,

- trust continuity compromised,

- architectural integrity unsafe.

## Mandatory INVALID Conditions

Containment doctrine becomes INVALID automatically if:

- containment pathways authorize execution,

- containment bypasses governance review,

- containment mutates production state,

- containment suppresses escalation states,

- containment bypasses rollback requirements,

- containment bypasses reconciliation requirements,

- containment downgrades governance classifications automatically,

- containment bypasses prototype review corridors,

- containment bypasses certification governance,

- containment bypasses readiness attestation governance,

- containment bypasses trust stratification governance,

- or containment becomes orchestration-capable.

## Required Governance Attachments

All future containment systems must eventually attach to:

- transaction lifecycle references,

- rollback semantics,

- reconciliation semantics,

- audit traceability,

- runtime isolation guarantees,

- target classifications,

- execution eligibility classifications,

- escalation classifications,

- governance persistence references,

- governance lineage references,

- prototype review corridor references,

- prototype certification references,

- capability certification references,

- readiness attestation references,

- trust stratification references.

## Carry-Forward Invariants

- Preview remains read-only.

- Renderer remains non-authoritative.

- Sandbox remains isolated.

- Matilda remains governance-only validation.

- Governance remains higher authority than execution eligibility.

- No mutation occurs without future governed implementation.

## Phase 746 Limitation

Phase 746 may define containment doctrine architecture only.

No execution runtime, orchestration engine, mutation-capable containment system, or production execution bridge may be implemented.

## Locked Conclusion

Future execution containment systems must remain permanently separated from mutation-capable execution authority so containment review, trust review, certification review, readiness review, rollback review, reconciliation review, governance review, and execution eligibility review cannot silently evolve into uncontrolled orchestration infrastructure.
