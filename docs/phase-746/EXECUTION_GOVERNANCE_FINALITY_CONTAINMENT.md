
# Phase 746 Execution Governance Finality Containment

## Status

Planning-only governance-finality containment architecture document.

This file does not implement execution authority.

## Purpose

Define the governance-finality containment architecture required before any future execution-capable proposal could classify final governance stop states, irreversible survivability conclusions, and terminal containment boundaries without activating runtime mutation.

## Locked Principle

Governance finality is not execution authority.

A system may classify finality conditions without gaining mutation, orchestration, rollback, or reconciliation authority.

## Governance Finality Containment Definition

Execution governance-finality containment refers to future governance pathways responsible for:

- classifying final governance stop states,

- classifying irreversible survivability conclusions,

- classifying terminal containment boundaries,

- classifying final escalation closure constraints,

- classifying final rollback prohibition conditions,

- classifying final reconciliation prohibition conditions,

- classifying final restoration prohibition conditions,

- classifying final recursion containment boundaries,

- classifying final drift-arbitration containment boundaries,

- classifying final equilibrium-stabilization limits,

- classifying final cohesion-recovery limits,

- classifying final fracture-containment boundaries,

- classifying final collapse-prevention limits,

- classifying final persistence-variance thresholds,

- classifying final variance thresholds,

- classifying final invariance thresholds,

- and classifying final execution-eligibility revocation states

without activating execution authority.

## Allowed Governance Finality Activities

Future governance-finality containment systems may:

- classify final governance stop states,

- classify irreversible invalidation conditions,

- classify final survivability conclusions,

- classify terminal containment boundaries,

- classify non-restorable governance states,

- classify final escalation requirements,

- generate non-authoritative finality reports,

- escalate finality review conditions.

## Explicitly Forbidden Governance Finality Activities

Governance-finality containment systems must NOT:

- mutate runtime state,

- authorize execution,

- invoke orchestration,

- invoke rollback execution,

- invoke reconciliation mutation,

- self-resolve final governance states automatically,

- self-authorize terminal pathways,

- bypass governance review,

- suppress escalation pathways,

- infer legitimacy from finality analysis alone,

- reactivate invalidated execution eligibility,

- downgrade finality classifications automatically,

- become orchestration-capable,

- or activate production execution authority.

## Required Governance Finality States

Future governance-finality containment systems must eventually support:

### 1. FINALITY_REVIEW_PENDING

Meaning:

- finality classification initiated,

- governance continuity preserved,

- execution authority absent.

### 2. FINALITY_REVIEW_REQUIRED

Meaning:

- terminal governance classification requires review.

### 3. FINALITY_QUARANTINED

Meaning:

- finality uncertainty detected,

- governance continuity preserved,

- investigation required.

### 4. FINALITY_ESCALATED

Meaning:

- terminal governance risk requires higher governance review.

### 5. FINALITY_CONFIRMED

Meaning:

- final governance stop state confirmed,

- execution eligibility revoked where applicable,

- containment boundary preserved.

### 6. INVALID_FINALITY_STATE

Meaning:

- finality classification integrity compromised,

- governance continuity unstable,

- execution eligibility revoked.

## Mandatory INVALID Conditions

Governance-finality containment becomes INVALID automatically if:

- finality pathways authorize execution,

- finality pathways mutate production state,

- finality pathways self-resolve governance continuity automatically,

- finality pathways bypass governance review,

- finality pathways suppress escalation pathways,

- finality pathways infer legitimacy from finality analysis alone,

- finality pathways reactivate invalidated execution eligibility,

- finality pathways downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- finality pathways become orchestration-capable,

- or finality pathways activate production execution authority.

## Phase 746 Limitation

Phase 746 may define governance-finality containment architecture only.

No execution runtime, orchestration engine, mutation-capable governance-finality containment system, or production execution bridge may be implemented.

## Locked Conclusion

Future execution governance-finality containment systems must remain permanently separated from mutation-capable execution authority so finality review, recursion-containment review, drift-arbitration review, equilibrium-stabilization review, cohesion-recovery review, fracture-containment review, restoration-topology review, collapse-prevention review, persistence-variance review, variance review, invariance review, rollback review, reconciliation review, governance review, and execution eligibility review cannot silently evolve into uncontrolled orchestration infrastructure.
