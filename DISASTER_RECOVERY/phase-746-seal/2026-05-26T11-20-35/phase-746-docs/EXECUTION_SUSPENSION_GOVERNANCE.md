
# Phase 746 Execution Suspension Governance

## Status

Planning-only execution suspension governance architecture document.

This file does not implement execution authority.

## Purpose

Define the governance suspension architecture required before any future execution-capable proposal could claim bounded suspension survivability.

## Locked Principle

Suspension governance is not execution authorization.

A system may classify governance-preserving suspension survivability without gaining runtime mutation authority.

## Execution Suspension Governance Definition

Execution suspension governance refers to future governance pathways responsible for:

- classifying governance-preserving suspension behavior,

- classifying rollback suspension continuity,

- classifying reconciliation suspension continuity,

- classifying escalation suspension continuity,

- classifying quarantine suspension continuity,

- classifying containment suspension continuity,

- classifying interruption suspension continuity,

- classifying recovery suspension continuity,

- classifying re-entry suspension continuity,

- and classifying trust-preserving deferred review survivability

without activating execution authority.

## Allowed Suspension Governance Activities

Future suspension governance systems may:

- validate governance suspension survivability,

- validate rollback/reconciliation suspension linkage,

- validate escalation suspension continuity,

- validate governance persistence suspension continuity,

- validate lineage survivability during suspension,

- validate quarantine suspension continuity,

- validate containment suspension continuity,

- validate interruption suspension continuity,

- validate recovery suspension continuity,

- validate re-entry suspension continuity,

- validate continuity certification survivability,

- generate non-authoritative suspension governance reports,

- escalate governance concerns.

## Explicitly Forbidden Suspension Governance Activities

Suspension governance systems must NOT:

- mutate runtime state,

- authorize execution,

- invoke rollback execution,

- invoke reconciliation mutation,

- bypass governance review,

- self-authorize orchestration,

- promote sandbox state into production,

- suppress escalation states,

- downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- infer legitimacy from suspension recovery alone,

- bypass prototype review corridors,

- bypass certification governance,

- bypass readiness attestation governance,

- bypass trust stratification governance,

- bypass containment governance,

- bypass continuity certification governance,

- bypass interruption governance,

- bypass recovery governance,

- or bypass re-entry governance.

## Required Suspension Governance States

Future suspension governance systems must eventually support:

### 1. SUSPENDED

Meaning:

- bounded suspension classification initiated,

- governance continuity preserved,

- execution authority still absent.

## 2. SUSPENSION_REVIEW_REQUIRED

Meaning:

- governance review required before suspension continuation.

## 3. SUSPENSION_QUARANTINED

Meaning:

- suspension isolated,

- governance continuity preserved,

- investigation required.

## 4. SUSPENSION_ESCALATED

Meaning:

- suspension concerns require higher governance review classification.

## 5. SUSPENSION_CONTAINED

Meaning:

- containment integrity preserved during suspension,

- governance continuity maintained.

## 6. INVALID_SUSPENSION_STATE

Meaning:

- governance continuity compromised,

- suspension survivability unsafe,

- execution eligibility revoked.

## Phase 746 Limitation

Phase 746 may define suspension governance architecture only.

No execution runtime, orchestration engine, mutation-capable suspension system, or production execution bridge may be implemented.

## Locked Conclusion

Future execution suspension governance systems must remain permanently separated from mutation-capable execution authority so suspension review, re-entry review, recovery review, interruption review, continuity review, containment review, trust review, certification review, readiness review, rollback review, reconciliation review, governance review, and execution eligibility review cannot silently evolve into uncontrolled orchestration infrastructure.
