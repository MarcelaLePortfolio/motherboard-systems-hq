
# Phase 746 Execution Recovery Governance

## Status

Planning-only execution recovery governance architecture document.

This file does not implement execution authority.

## Purpose

Define the governance recovery architecture required before any future execution-capable proposal could claim bounded recovery survivability.

## Locked Principle

Recovery governance is not execution authorization.

A system may classify governance-preserving recovery survivability without gaining runtime mutation authority.

## Execution Recovery Governance Definition

Execution recovery governance refers to future governance pathways responsible for:

- classifying governance-preserving recovery behavior,

- classifying rollback recovery continuity,

- classifying reconciliation recovery continuity,

- classifying escalation recovery continuity,

- classifying quarantine recovery continuity,

- classifying containment recovery continuity,

- classifying interruption recovery continuity,

- and classifying trust-preserving restoration survivability

without activating execution authority.

## Allowed Recovery Governance Activities

Future recovery governance systems may:

- validate governance recovery survivability,

- validate rollback/reconciliation recovery linkage,

- validate escalation recovery continuity,

- validate governance persistence recovery continuity,

- validate lineage survivability during recovery,

- validate quarantine recovery continuity,

- validate containment recovery continuity,

- validate interruption recovery continuity,

- validate continuity certification survivability,

- generate non-authoritative recovery governance reports,

- escalate governance concerns.

## Explicitly Forbidden Recovery Governance Activities

Recovery governance systems must NOT:

- mutate runtime state,

- authorize execution,

- invoke rollback execution,

- invoke reconciliation mutation,

- bypass governance review,

- self-authorize orchestration,

- promote sandbox state into production,

- suppress escalation states,

- downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- infer legitimacy from recovery success alone,

- bypass prototype review corridors,

- bypass certification governance,

- bypass readiness attestation governance,

- bypass trust stratification governance,

- bypass containment governance,

- bypass continuity certification governance,

- or bypass interruption governance.

## Required Recovery Governance States

Future recovery governance systems must eventually support:

### 1. RECOVERY_PENDING

Meaning:

- bounded recovery classification initiated,

- governance continuity preserved,

- execution authority still absent.

## 2. RECOVERY_REVIEW_REQUIRED

Meaning:

- governance review required before recovery classification continuation.

## 3. RECOVERY_QUARANTINED

Meaning:

- recovery isolated,

- governance continuity preserved,

- investigation required.

## 4. RECOVERY_ESCALATED

Meaning:

- recovery concerns require higher governance review classification.

## 5. RECOVERY_CONTAINED

Meaning:

- containment integrity preserved during recovery,

- governance continuity maintained.

## 6. INVALID_RECOVERY_STATE

Meaning:

- governance continuity compromised,

- recovery survivability unsafe,

- execution eligibility revoked.

## Mandatory INVALID Conditions

Recovery governance becomes INVALID automatically if:

- recovery pathways authorize execution,

- recovery pathways bypass governance review,

- recovery pathways mutate production state,

- recovery pathways suppress escalation states,

- recovery pathways bypass rollback requirements,

- recovery pathways bypass reconciliation requirements,

- recovery pathways downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- recovery pathways bypass prototype review corridors,

- recovery pathways bypass certification governance,

- recovery pathways bypass readiness attestation governance,

- recovery pathways bypass trust stratification governance,

- recovery pathways bypass containment governance,

- recovery pathways bypass continuity certification governance,

- recovery pathways bypass interruption governance,

- or recovery pathways become orchestration-capable.

## Carry-Forward Invariants

- Preview remains read-only.

- Renderer remains non-authoritative.

- Sandbox remains isolated.

- Matilda remains governance-only validation.

- Governance remains higher authority than execution eligibility.

- No mutation occurs without future governed implementation.

## Phase 746 Limitation

Phase 746 may define recovery governance architecture only.

No execution runtime, orchestration engine, mutation-capable recovery system, or production execution bridge may be implemented.

## Locked Conclusion

Future execution recovery governance systems must remain permanently separated from mutation-capable execution authority so recovery review, interruption review, continuity review, containment review, trust review, certification review, readiness review, rollback review, reconciliation review, governance review, and execution eligibility review cannot silently evolve into uncontrolled orchestration infrastructure.
