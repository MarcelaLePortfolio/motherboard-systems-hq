
# Phase 746 Execution Governance Continuity Exhaustion

## Status

Planning-only governance-continuity exhaustion architecture document.

This file does not implement execution authority.

## Purpose

Define the governance-continuity exhaustion architecture required before any future execution-capable proposal could classify governance continuity depletion conditions, governance survivability exhaustion pathways, and governance persistence exhaustion thresholds without activating runtime mutation authority.

## Locked Principle

Governance continuity exhaustion classification is not execution authority.

A system may classify governance continuity exhaustion conditions without gaining mutation, orchestration, rollback, reconciliation, restoration, or execution authority.

## Governance Continuity Exhaustion Definition

Execution governance continuity exhaustion refers to future governance pathways responsible for:

- classifying governance continuity depletion,

- classifying governance survivability exhaustion,

- classifying governance persistence exhaustion,

- classifying governance restoration depletion,

- classifying governance integrity exhaustion,

- classifying governance stabilization exhaustion,

- classifying governance containment exhaustion,

- classifying governance reconciliation fatigue,

- classifying governance rollback exhaustion,

- classifying governance drift accumulation saturation,

- classifying governance fracture persistence,

- classifying governance recursion exhaustion,

- classifying governance attrition escalation,

- classifying governance equilibrium destabilization,

- classifying governance exhaustion escalation pathways,

- and classifying governance survivability depletion thresholds

without activating execution authority.

## Allowed Governance Continuity Exhaustion Activities

Future governance-continuity exhaustion systems may:

- classify governance exhaustion conditions,

- classify continuity depletion trajectories,

- classify survivability exhaustion thresholds,

- classify governance restoration instability,

- generate non-authoritative governance exhaustion reports,

- escalate governance exhaustion review conditions.

## Explicitly Forbidden Governance Continuity Exhaustion Activities

Governance-continuity exhaustion systems must NOT:

- mutate runtime state,

- authorize execution,

- invoke orchestration,

- invoke rollback execution,

- invoke reconciliation mutation,

- self-enforce exhaustion outcomes automatically,

- self-authorize survivability decisions,

- bypass governance review,

- suppress escalation pathways,

- infer legitimacy from exhaustion analysis alone,

- reactivate exhausted execution eligibility,

- downgrade governance exhaustion classifications automatically,

- become orchestration-capable,

- or activate production execution authority.

- governance exhaustion integrity compromised,
