
# Phase 746 Execution Reentry Governance

## Status

Planning-only execution re-entry governance architecture document.

This file does not implement execution authority.

## Purpose

Define the governance re-entry architecture required before any future execution-capable proposal could claim bounded re-entry survivability.

## Locked Principle

Re-entry governance is not execution authorization.

A system may classify governance-preserving re-entry survivability without gaining runtime mutation authority.

## Execution Re-entry Governance Definition

Execution re-entry governance refers to future governance pathways responsible for:

- classifying governance-preserving re-entry behavior,

- classifying rollback re-entry continuity,

- classifying reconciliation re-entry continuity,

- classifying escalation re-entry continuity,

- classifying quarantine re-entry continuity,

- classifying containment re-entry continuity,

- classifying interruption recovery re-entry continuity,

- and classifying trust-preserving restoration re-entry survivability

without activating execution authority.

## Allowed Re-entry Governance Activities

Future re-entry governance systems may:

- validate governance re-entry survivability,

- validate rollback/reconciliation re-entry linkage,

- validate escalation re-entry continuity,

- validate governance persistence re-entry continuity,

- validate lineage survivability during re-entry,

- validate quarantine re-entry continuity,

- validate containment re-entry continuity,

- validate interruption recovery re-entry continuity,

- validate continuity certification survivability,

- validate recovery governance survivability,

- generate non-authoritative re-entry governance reports,

- escalate governance concerns.

## Explicitly Forbidden Re-entry Governance Activities

Re-entry governance systems must NOT:

- mutate runtime state,

- authorize execution,

- invoke rollback execution,

- invoke reconciliation mutation,

- bypass governance review,

- self-authorize orchestration,

- promote sandbox state into production,

- suppress escalation states,

- downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- infer legitimacy from recovery success alone,

- bypass prototype review corridors,

- bypass certification governance,

- bypass readiness attestation governance,

- bypass trust stratification governance,

- bypass containment governance,

- bypass continuity certification governance,

- bypass interruption governance,

- or bypass recovery governance.

## Required Re-entry Governance States

Future re-entry governance systems must eventually support:

### 1. REENTRY_PENDING

Meaning:

- bounded re-entry classification initiated,

- governance continuity preserved,

- execution authority still absent.

## 2. REENTRY_REVIEW_REQUIRED

Meaning:

- governance review required before re-entry continuation.

## 3. REENTRY_QUARANTINED

Meaning:

- re-entry isolated,

- governance continuity preserved,

- investigation required.

## 4. REENTRY_ESCALATED

Meaning:

- re-entry concerns require higher governance review classification.

## 5. REENTRY_CONTAINED

Meaning:

- containment integrity preserved during re-entry,

- governance continuity maintained.

## 6. INVALID_REENTRY_STATE

Meaning:

- governance continuity compromised,

- re-entry survivability unsafe,

- execution eligibility revoked.

## Mandatory INVALID Conditions

Re-entry governance becomes INVALID automatically if:

- re-entry pathways authorize execution,

- re-entry pathways bypass governance review,

- re-entry pathways mutate production state,

- re-entry pathways suppress escalation states,

- re-entry pathways bypass rollback requirements,

- re-entry pathways bypass reconciliation requirements,

- re-entry pathways downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- re-entry pathways bypass prototype review corridors,

- re-entry pathways bypass certification governance,

- re-entry pathways bypass readiness attestation governance,

- re-entry pathways bypass trust stratification governance,

- re-entry pathways bypass containment governance,

- re-entry pathways bypass continuity certification governance,

- re-entry pathways bypass interruption governance,

- re-entry pathways bypass recovery governance,

- or re-entry pathways become orchestration-capable.

## Carry-Forward Invariants

- Preview remains read-only.

- Renderer remains non-authoritative.

- Sandbox remains isolated.

- Matilda remains governance-only validation.

- Governance remains higher authority than execution eligibility.

- No mutation occurs without future governed implementation.

## Phase 746 Limitation

Phase 746 may define re-entry governance architecture only.

No execution runtime, orchestration engine, mutation-capable re-entry system, or production execution bridge may be implemented.

## Locked Conclusion

Future execution re-entry governance systems must remain permanently separated from mutation-capable execution authority so re-entry review, recovery review, interruption review, continuity review, containment review, trust review, certification review, readiness review, rollback review, reconciliation review, governance review, and execution eligibility review cannot silently evolve into uncontrolled orchestration infrastructure.
