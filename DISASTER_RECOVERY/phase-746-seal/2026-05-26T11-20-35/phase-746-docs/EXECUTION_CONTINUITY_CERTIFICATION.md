
# Phase 746 Execution Continuity Certification

## Status

Planning-only execution continuity certification architecture document.

This file does not implement execution authority.

## Purpose

Define the governance continuity certification architecture required before any future execution-capable proposal could claim bounded continuity survivability.

## Locked Principle

Continuity certification is not execution authorization.

A system may become continuity-certified for governance review without gaining runtime mutation authority.

## Execution Continuity Certification Definition

Execution continuity certification refers to future governance pathways responsible for:

- certifying governance continuity survivability,

- certifying rollback continuity survivability,

- certifying reconciliation continuity survivability,

- certifying escalation continuity survivability,

- certifying lineage continuity survivability,

- certifying quarantine continuity survivability,

- certifying containment continuity survivability,

- and certifying trust continuity survivability

without activating execution authority.

## Allowed Continuity Certification Activities

Future continuity certification systems may:

- validate governance survivability continuity,

- validate rollback/reconciliation continuity linkage,

- validate escalation survivability continuity,

- validate audit lineage survivability,

- validate governance persistence survivability,

- validate prototype/capability certification continuity,

- validate readiness attestation continuity,

- validate trust stratification continuity,

- validate containment continuity guarantees,

- generate non-authoritative continuity certification reports,

- escalate governance concerns.

## Explicitly Forbidden Continuity Certification Activities

Continuity certification systems must NOT:

- mutate runtime state,

- authorize execution,

- invoke rollback execution,

- invoke reconciliation mutation,

- bypass governance review,

- self-authorize orchestration,

- promote sandbox state into production,

- suppress escalation states,

- downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- infer legitimacy from interruption survival,

- bypass prototype review corridors,

- bypass certification governance,

- bypass readiness attestation governance,

- bypass trust stratification governance,

- or bypass containment governance.

## Required Continuity Certification Levels

Future continuity certification systems must eventually support:

### 1. NON_CERTIFIED

Meaning:

- continuity survivability incomplete,

- continuity review not eligible.

## 2. REVIEW_CERTIFIED

Meaning:

- governance continuity review satisfied,

- bounded continuity survivability established,

- execution authority still absent.

## 3. CONTINUITY_CERTIFIED

Meaning:

- governance continuity survivability validated,

- rollback/reconciliation survivability validated,

- runtime isolation survivability validated,

- execution authority still NOT granted.

## 4. ESCALATED_FOR_REVIEW

Meaning:

- governance concerns require higher continuity review classification.

## 5. QUARANTINED

Meaning:

- continuity preservation active,

- certification blocked pending investigation.

## 6. INVALID_CONTINUITY_STATE

Meaning:

- governance continuity compromised,

- certification survivability unsafe,

- execution eligibility revoked.

## Mandatory INVALID Conditions

Continuity certification becomes INVALID automatically if:

- continuity pathways authorize execution,

- continuity pathways bypass governance review,

- continuity pathways mutate production state,

- continuity pathways suppress escalation states,

- continuity pathways bypass rollback requirements,

- continuity pathways bypass reconciliation requirements,

- continuity pathways downgrade governance classifications automatically,

- continuity pathways bypass prototype review corridors,

- continuity pathways bypass certification governance,

- continuity pathways bypass readiness attestation governance,

- continuity pathways bypass trust stratification governance,

- continuity pathways bypass containment governance,

- or continuity pathways become orchestration-capable.

## Required Governance Attachments

All future continuity certification systems must eventually attach to:

- transaction lifecycle references,

- rollback semantics,

- reconciliation semantics,

- audit traceability,

- runtime isolation guarantees,

- target classifications,

- execution eligibility classifications,

- escalation classifications,

- governance persistence references,

- governance lineage references,

- prototype review corridor references,

- prototype certification references,

- capability certification references,

- readiness attestation references,

- trust stratification references,

- containment doctrine references.

## Carry-Forward Invariants

- Preview remains read-only.

- Renderer remains non-authoritative.

- Sandbox remains isolated.

- Matilda remains governance-only validation.

- Governance remains higher authority than execution eligibility.

- No mutation occurs without future governed implementation.

## Phase 746 Limitation

Phase 746 may define continuity certification architecture only.

No execution runtime, orchestration engine, mutation-capable continuity certification system, or production execution bridge may be implemented.

## Locked Conclusion

Future execution continuity certification systems must remain permanently separated from mutation-capable execution authority so continuity review, containment review, trust review, certification review, readiness review, rollback review, reconciliation review, governance review, and execution eligibility review cannot silently evolve into uncontrolled orchestration infrastructure.
