
# Execution Corridor Findings (Stabilized)

## Status

Execution Corridor is considered architecturally stabilized.

Remaining work consists primarily of artifact schemas rather than ontology discovery.

---

# Constitutional Invariants

## Intent Sovereignty

Only the user originates intent.

The organization may interpret intent, operationalize intent, and fulfill intent.

The organization may not originate intent.

---

## Constituent Authority

Only the user may evolve the organization.

The organization may recommend organizational changes.

The organization may not enact organizational evolution autonomously.

---

## Evidence Preservation

The organization creates new evidence.

The organization does not rewrite organizational history.

Determinations become evidence artifacts.

New evidence produces successor artifacts.

Prior evidence remains historical truth.

---

## Preview Before Commitment

The organization may reason privately.

The organization may not create consequential commitments privately.

Pattern:

Determination

↓

Preview

↓

Commitment

Examples:

Matilda Interpretation

↓

Intent Preview

↓

Package

Execution Plan

↓

Execution Preview

↓

Execution

# Atlas Invariants

Atlas is Organizational Intelligence.

Atlas is Organizational Memory.

Atlas is not an enabling authority.

Atlas is not a governance authority.

Atlas is not a responsibility authority.

Atlas may improve decisions.

Atlas may not be required for decisions.

Bootstrap Test:

If Atlas disappears and the organization cannot function, Atlas has acquired excessive authority.

Supporting Departments answer:

Can I fulfill my assigned responsibility?

Outputs:

Contribution Fulfillment Plan

or

Contribution Executability Failure

Contribution Accountability

Supporting Departments do not own:

# Owner Department

Owner Department answers:

Can all assigned responsibilities be integrated into a coherent fulfillment path?

Outputs:

or

Integration Failure

Owner Department owns:

Integration Accountability

Owner Department does not own:

Supporting Department Fulfillment Methods

# Escalation Boundary

Execution autonomy extends until fulfillment requires modification of a higher-layer artifact.

When a higher-layer artifact must change:

Escalation is required.

Escalation target is determined by artifact ownership.

Question:

Which artifact must change?

Then:

Who owns that artifact?

That authority becomes the escalation target.

Artifacts are not mutable organizational state.

# Organizational Ontology Finding

The organization is a lineage-preserving evidence system.

Authorities do not rewrite organizational history.

Authorities contribute new evidence to organizational history.

Organizational truth is derived from the most recent valid evidence chain.
