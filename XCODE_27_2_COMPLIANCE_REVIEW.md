# Xcode 27.2 — Compliance Review Register

Status: DOCUMENTATION / FUTURE REVIEW REQUIRED
Agreement: Xcode and Apple SDKs Agreement, EA2002, dated 06/08/2026
Xcode: 27.2 (27B52)
License acceptance: November 6, 2026
System: Motherboard Systems HQ / Executive Agent Suite

## 1. Current Development Boundary

- Xcode is authorized for applicable local development and testing.
- Preserve the existing system architecture and governance boundaries.
- License acceptance does not authorize production deployment, distribution, or repository modifications.
- Xcode 16.4 remains installed alongside Xcode 27.2.
- The system-wide developer directory remains unchanged unless separately authorized.

## 2. AI Model Restrictions — REVIEW BEFORE INTEGRATION

Agreement references: Sections 2.2.C and 2.11.C.

- Do not use output generated by an Apple model to train, fine-tune, or improve another AI model.
- Do not programmatically access Apple models except as expressly permitted.
- Review the Foundation Models Framework Acceptable Use Requirements before integrating that framework.
- Maintain provenance for AI-generated content used in agent development.
- Do not assume these restrictions automatically apply to every non-Apple AI model or output; evaluate the actual source and applicable terms.

Review trigger:
Before connecting Apple Foundation Models or Apple-generated outputs to Cade, Effie, Ellis, Chief of Staff, or any agent-improvement pipeline.

## 3. Proprietary Constructs and Third-Party Components

Agreement reference: Section 2.11.D.

- Preserve the separation between internal-use constructs and market-facing products.
- Do not redistribute licensed Mirror System constructs without the necessary rights.
- Market-facing agents must remain self-contained and independent of nonredistributable internal constructs.
- Review third-party component licenses, permissions, security, data handling, and intellectual-property obligations.
- Apple's permission to integrate a third-party component does not replace that component's own license.

Review trigger:
Before agent generation, external demonstrations, customer delivery, commercial licensing, or third-party integration.

## 4. Apple Platform and Distribution Restrictions

Agreement references: Sections 2.2.A–B and 2.4.

- Local development does not automatically authorize third-party iOS distribution.
- Internal device testing must comply with authorized-test-unit and provisioning restrictions.
- Review the Apple Developer Program License Agreement before distributing applicable iOS applications.
- Review App Store requirements separately when applicable.
- macOS distribution has different provisions; evaluate the intended distribution channel before release.

Review trigger:
Before installing Ellis on test devices, inviting external testers, publishing applications, or distributing commercial products.

## 5. Privacy, Security, and Device Data

Agreement references: Sections 2.11.E–F and 8.2.

- Respect Apple system permission dialogs and security controls.
- Review collection, transmission, retention, and disclosure of personal information.
- Device provisioning can involve Apple Account and hardware identifiers.
- Review diagnostic-data preferences and third-party data handling.
- Preserve existing authentication, authorization, and execution boundaries.

Review trigger:
Before physical-device testing, enabling telemetry, processing customer information, or deploying integrations.

## 6. Intellectual Property and Generated Code

Agreement references: Sections 2.6 and 2.11.F.

- Apple does not claim ownership of applications solely through this agreement.
- Generated code and content remain the developer's responsibility to test and review.
- Verify intellectual-property rights, attribution, and relevant open-source licenses.
- Do not redistribute Apple SDKs, proprietary software, or restricted assets outside permitted uses.

Review trigger:
Before incorporating generated code or licensed assets into commercial deliverables.

## 7. Operational and Legal Exposure

Agreement references: Sections 2.8, 4, 5, 6, and 7.

- Apple may modify or discontinue software, services, and APIs.
- The agreement includes indemnification obligations and warranty disclaimers.
- The agreement limits Apple's liability.
- Noncompliance may terminate license rights.
- Maintain independent backups and avoid assuming ongoing service availability.
- Obtain qualified legal review before significant commercial distribution or contractual commitments.

## 8. Governance and Authorization

- This register is a reminder and review checklist, not legal clearance.
- Preserve evidence-first investigation and rollback discipline.
- Do not expand scope based solely on newly available Xcode capabilities.
- Require separate authorization for installation changes, repository modifications, device deployment, and commercial release.
- Recheck current Apple agreements and applicable third-party licenses at each relevant review trigger.
