
PLANNING AUTHORIZATION DOCTRINE ELEVATION FINDING

Finding Status: ARCHITECTURAL INVARIANT CANDIDATE

Question:

Should residual risk acceptance remain a Preview Builder-local finding, or should it be elevated into higher-level Planning and Authorization Doctrine?

Reconciled Direction:

This finding should be treated as a candidate architectural invariant.

It explains why the Preview Builder, Future-State Review, Risk Visibility, Review Result Artifacts, Authorization Artifacts, and audit reconstruction lifecycle exist.

Core Doctrine:

Primary Objective:

Achieve intended future state.

Secondary Objective:

Minimize unnecessary risk.

Tertiary Objective:

Expose residual risk to the user.

Quaternary Objective:

Require explicit human acceptance of residual risk before authorization.

Meaning:

The system should not optimize primarily for inaction.

The system should not pursue intended outcomes recklessly.

The system should not hide residual risk.

The system should not treat approval as meaningful authorization unless residual risk has been disclosed and consciously accepted.

Product Meaning:

Risk visibility gives authorization a unique approval quality.

The user is not merely approving the same proposal again.

The user is moving from:

- understanding the proposal

to:

- accepting responsibility for proceeding with known residual risk

Governance Meaning:

Authorization exists because residual risk ownership must transfer to the appropriate human authority before execution.

Risk disclosure is therefore both:

- an informed decision-making feature

- a responsibility-boundary feature

Relationship To Lifecycle:

Interpretation Review establishes shared understanding.

Future-State Review establishes shared intent.

Risk Disclosure establishes visible residual risk.

Authorization establishes conscious acceptance of residual risk.

Execution proceeds only after authority is explicitly granted for the exact proposal.

Relationship To Prior Findings:

This finding reinforces:

- Interpretation-First Approval

- Future-State Review

- Risk Visibility and Disclosure

- Residual Risk Acceptance Doctrine

- Proposal Identity

- Proposal Lineage

- Review Validity

- Authorization Validity

- Execution Lineage Binding

- Lifecycle Audit Reconstruction

Invariant Candidate:

No execution authorization should be treated as complete unless:

- the intended future state is represented

- unnecessary risk has been minimized

- residual risk has been visibly disclosed

- the responsible human authority has accepted that residual risk

- authorization references the exact proposal being authorized

Scope Boundary:

This finding does not authorize:

- implementation

- UI work

- approval system construction

- Preview Builder construction

- Review View Builder construction

- mutation

- shell execution

- autonomous execution
