
SERVED DASHBOARD ANCHOR MISMATCH FINDING

Finding Status: CONFIRMED

The served /dashboard route points to public/dashboard.html.

public/dashboard.html loads:

- js/dashboard-bundle-entry.js

dashboard-bundle-entry.js currently imports only baseline dashboard/runtime modules.

It does not import:

- phase530_visible_panels_bridge.js

- phase565_recent_tasks_wire.js

- phase61_tabs_workspace.js

- project-visual-output.js

Those modern/anchored UI scripts are present on public/index.html, not the served /dashboard surface.

Conclusion:

Before adding a Planning Preview card, the authoritative served UI surface must be reconciled.

Do not patch planning preview UI into public/index.html unless /dashboard is intentionally moved to public/index.html or the relevant anchored scripts are intentionally restored to public/dashboard.html.

Next safe action:

Inspect prior dashboard anchor findings and determine whether public/index.html or public/dashboard.html is the intended current authoritative dashboard.
