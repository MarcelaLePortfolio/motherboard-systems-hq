FUTURE AUTHORIZATION ARTIFACT SURFACES FINDING

Finding Status: DISCOVERY NEXT STEP

Context:

The doctrine-to-architecture consistency verdict confirmed that the current governed execution architecture remains safely before runtime execution authorization.

The key remaining gap is that residual-risk acceptance is documented as doctrine but not yet implemented as a lifecycle artifact.

Question:

What minimum future artifact surfaces are required to support proposal-bound authorization without expanding execution authority prematurely?

Candidate Future Artifact Surfaces:

1. Proposal Risk Surface

Purpose:

Represent the risk value attached to the exact proposal.

Minimum fields:

- proposal_id

- risk_level

- risk_summary

- risk_details_ref

- generated_at

2. Residual Risk Disclosure Surface

Purpose:

Record that residual risk was visibly disclosed at the decision point.

Minimum fields:

- proposal_id

- disclosed_risk_level

- disclosed_risk_summary

- disclosure_surface

- disclosed_at

- disclosure_version

3. Human Risk Acceptance Surface

Purpose:

Record whether the responsible human authority accepted the disclosed residual risk.

Minimum fields:

- proposal_id

- accepted_by

- accepted_at

- accepted_risk_level

- acceptance_statement

- acceptance_scope

- acceptance_expires_at

4. Proposal-Bound Authorization Surface

Purpose:

Record whether execution authority was granted for the exact proposal.

Minimum fields:

- proposal_id

- authorization_id

- authorization_state

- authorized_by

- authorized_at

- authorization_scope

- authorization_expires_at

- review_result_ref

- risk_acceptance_ref

Boundary:

These surfaces are lifecycle artifacts.

They should not be collapsed into the Canonical Preview Artifact.

They should not mutate the Canonical Preview Artifact.

They should not authorize any changed proposal automatically.

Architecture Rule:

Proposal identity anchors validity.

Risk disclosure supports informed consent.

Human risk acceptance supports responsibility clarity.

Authorization grants or denies execution authority only for the exact proposal.

Scope Boundary:

This finding does not authorize:

- implementation

- UI work

- approval system construction

- Preview Builder construction

- Review View Builder construction

- mutation

- shell execution

- autonomous execution
