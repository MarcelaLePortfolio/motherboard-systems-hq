
RISK VISIBILITY AND DISCLOSURE FINDING

Finding Status: RECONCILED

Question:

Should risk appear immediately in the review surface, or only as optional expandable detail?

Reconciled Direction:

Risk value should appear immediately.

Risk details should be expandable.

Meaning:

The user should not have to expand a section to learn that a proposal carries risk.

The user should be able to expand details to understand why the risk value was assigned.

Review Surface Pattern:

Immediate:

- interpretation summary

- future-state summary

- risk value

Expandable:

- risk explanation

- affected systems

- assumptions

- rollback considerations

- governance evidence

- supporting analysis

Governance Reason:

Risk visibility supports informed user review.

Risk detail supports deeper evaluation when needed.

This protects the user from unknowingly approving risky work.

This also protects the system from responsibility ambiguity by making risk disclosure visible at the decision point.

Boundary:

Risk existence is first-class attention.

Risk reasoning is progressive disclosure.

Risk value should not be hidden behind optional expansion.

Risk details do not need to dominate the default review surface.

Possible Contract Shape:

proposal_risk:

  level:

    LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH | CRITICAL

  summary:

    Short human-readable risk summary.

  details_ref:

    Optional reference to supporting risk analysis.

Review View Rule:

Show risk level immediately.

Show risk summary immediately when available.

Place detailed risk analysis behind expandable review details.

Scope Boundary:
