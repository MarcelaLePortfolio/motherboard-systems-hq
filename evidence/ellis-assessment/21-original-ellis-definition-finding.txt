
ORIGINAL ELLIS DEFINITION FINDING

Finding:

The original Ellis definition already included constituent authority, operational coordination, capability routing, ownership assignment, routing state, and multi-agent workflow coordination.

Evidence observed from introduction commits:

HEADQUARTERS_ORGANIZATIONAL_CHARTER at 36e50d1a defines:

- Operations Coordination

- Role: Ellis

- Authority Domain: Operational Assignment and Coordination

- Responsibilities:

  - Consume Envelope artifacts

  - Route work by capability

  - Assign ownership

  - Track operational status

  - Coordinate multi-agent workflows

- Produces:

  - Assignment

  - Ownership

  - Routing State

  - Coordination State

CAPABILITY_ROUTING_MODEL at 28551668 defines:

- Ellis as the stage after Envelope and before Capability Resolution / Assignment.

- Governance Validation derives capabilities.

- Governance Validation writes required capabilities into the Envelope.

- The Envelope does not contain actor assignments.

- Ellis owns capability resolution and assignment.

- Ellis consumes Required Capabilities, Operational Corridor, and Envelope State.

- Ellis determines Assignment, Ownership, and Routing State.

- Ellis routes by capabilities.

CANONICAL_ENVELOPE_SPECIFICATION at ca926930 defines:

- Ellis Routing as the stage after Envelope Created.

- Assigned as Envelope routed and assigned by Ellis.

- assignment_state is owned by Ellis with Ellis-only mutation authority.

- assigned_department is owned by Ellis with Ellis-only mutation authority.

Interpretation:

Ellis was originally defined as more than a mechanical router.

Ellis was originally defined as a named organizational authority for the coordination layer between Governance Validation and Operational Departments.

The multi-agent coordination responsibility was present at original introduction, not added later.

Current conclusion:

Ellis's constituent authority is foundational.

Ellis's assignment and coordination authority is foundational.

Ellis runtime remains unimplemented.

Ellis implementation shape remains deferred.

Future implementation-shape analysis should begin from the original Ellis definition rather than from existing router, scheduler, task-state, or worker-claim mechanics.
